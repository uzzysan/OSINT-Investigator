## Question

What licence does this ship under, and is the repository public?

The choice is load-bearing for a tool in this space and is cheapest to make
before the first public commit.

**Options to weigh**

- **AGPL-3.0** — keeps the tool and any hosted derivative open. Common for
  self-hosted investigative and security tooling. Deters commercial forks that
  would otherwise close it; also deters some corporate users from touching it.
- **MIT / Apache-2.0** — maximum adoption, including inside companies. Accepts
  that someone may build a closed hosted product on it.
- **Source-available (BSL, Elastic-style)** — open to read and self-host, closed
  to competing hosted services, converting to open after N years. Not OSI-open;
  some users will refuse it on principle.
- **Private repository** — no licence question yet, but also no contributors and
  no external scrutiny of a tool that handles sensitive data.

**Also to settle**

- Copyright holder: individual or an entity?
- Do third-party contributions need a CLA or DCO?
- Does the FollowTheMoney-derived schema material carry obligations? (FtM itself
  is MIT; we use its vocabulary, not its code — worth confirming.)
